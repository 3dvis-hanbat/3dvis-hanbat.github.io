Faculty

Principal Investigator

Seongmin Lee

Department of Artificial Intelligence Software, Hanbat National University
Assistant Professor

Seongmin Lee received a B.S. in Electronic and Electrical Engineering from Hongik University and an M.S. and Ph.D. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from Yonsei University. He was a postdoctoral researcher at the Yonsei University Industry–Academic Cooperation Foundation and joined Hanbat National University in March 2025.

His research interests include 3D reconstruction, visualization, and animation, generative AI for multimedia, and multimodal processing.
